- **"Targeted attacks on prominent Rustaceans": the vector is a video call, and the defence is waiting** (blog, 2026-09-17) [link](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/17/targeted-attacks-on-rustaceans/)
  Willison relays the crates security team's warning, quoting it: an ongoing campaign is targeting rust-lang members and owners of popular crates, trying to compromise devices and accounts in order to publish malware from them. The shape is always the same — a video call arranged around something positive, a job, a project, a contract — which is then used either to get the target to install something ("such as a purportedly missing audio codec") or to run a command the attacker has put on their clipboard. He points at last month's successful supply chain attack on the `arrayref` crate as the version of this that worked.
